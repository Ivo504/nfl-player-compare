About these numbers

  • Source: nflverse player stats, built from play-by-play, 1999 season to the present.
  • Totals can differ slightly from official league records. For example, Drew Brees's career passing yards add up to 80,428 here and 80,358 officially.
  • Career means 1999 onward (nflverse has no player stats before 1999). Players who were active before 1999 are marked, and their career totals are partial.
  • Games counts weeks with at least one recorded stat, so a player who dressed but recorded nothing is not counted.
  • Rate stats such as passer rating or yards per carry need a minimum sample (for example 50 attempts). Smaller samples show in italics and are not ranked.
  • Awards: AP MVP, Offensive and Defensive Player of the Year, Offensive and Defensive Rookie of the Year, Comeback Player of the Year, Walter Payton Man of the Year and Super Bowl MVP, 1999 season onward, from Pro Football Reference (1999 Comeback Player of the Year is not included). Offensive linemen are not in this dataset, so winners such as Will Shields and Andrew Whitworth do not appear.
  • Pro Bowl, first-team All-Pro and Hall of Fame counts come from nflverse draft data (Pro Football Reference) and cover each player's whole career, but only drafted players. Undrafted players show a dash even if they earned these honors.
  • QB wins and losses (quarterbacks only) credit the team result to the starting quarterback of each game, per nflverse schedules, so a QB who is pulled or relieved still gets the decision. Ties count half in win %. A quarterback who only came off the bench has no record.
  • Super Bowl wins and appearances count games in which the player recorded a stat.
  • Photos are not embedded because the page can only show images that ship with it. Each player card links to an ESPN profile with a photo and bio.
  • EPA and fantasy points use nflverse definitions. Postseason samples are small, so read them with care.
  • Luna, the assistant in the corner, answers questions from this page's data only, right in your browser. Nothing you ask is sent anywhere. Her voice comes from your device's built-in voices; a Spanish-language voice reading English gives her accent, and you can pick another voice in her panel. Voice input works in browsers that support speech recognition, such as Chrome, Edge and Safari.
  • Teams: 2000 season to the present, from nflverse team stats and schedules. Relocated franchises are combined under today's team (Raiders, Chargers, Rams), and the season log shows the abbreviation used that year. The Texans begin in 2002.
  • Team records, points and playoff results come from final scores. Yards and points allowed are the opponents' totals in those games; total yards are passing yards minus sack yards plus rushing yards, as the NFL counts them.
  • Team playoff and Super Bowl counts ignore the Games filter, so they show even when Regular season is picked.
